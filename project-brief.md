# My project brief
## The question
How are waterways distributed across Abeokuta South LGA, and which areas are most vulnerable to flooding due to their proximity to these waterways?
## The data I need for the project
- Abeokuta South LGA Boundary — https://gadm.org/data.html
- Waterways/drainage network — https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html
- Elevation (DEM) — https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
- Buildings/settlements — https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html
- Roads (optional) — https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com
- Population datasets (optional) — https://www.worldpop.org/
## What I would build
-I would build an interactive flood-vulnerability map of Abeokuta South LGA showing waterways and areas at different levels of proximity to them.
-The map would help users quickly identify locations that may require greater attention during flood-risk planning
